# Week 08 — Errors & Struggles

This week was Course 8 and Course 9 of the Google Cybersecurity
Certificate — Put It to Work: Prepare for Cybersecurity Jobs, and
Accelerate Your Job Search with AI. No technical struggle to report this
week, and I'm not going to manufacture one. This wasn't a debugging
week. This was the week the coursework stopped being about skills and
started being about deploying them.

---

## No Struggle to Debug — A Different Kind of Week

### What I Worked On

Course 8 covered when and how to escalate a security incident, how to
engage with the cybersecurity community, and how to actually apply for
roles and prepare for interviews. Course 9 shifted into using AI as a
job-search tool — exploring career paths with Career Dreamer, organizing
applications in Sheets, building a resume with Gemini, and running mock
interviews with Gemini Live.

### Why It Mattered

Knowing how to investigate an alert or write a detection query means
nothing if I can't communicate it in an interview, or know when a
finding is serious enough to escalate. This pair of courses closed that
gap — it's the difference between having the skills and being able to
show them to someone hiring for them.

**My Observation:**
Technical skill gets you the analysis. Communication and escalation
judgment get you the job.

---

## Week 8 Reflection on Struggles

Every week before this one had a syntax error, a backwards rule, an
unescaped character, or a step skipped too early. This week didn't have
any of that, and that's worth noting rather than papering over. The
first seven weeks were about whether the tool did what I told it to.
This week was about whether *I* could explain, out loud, why any of it
mattered — and that turned out to be a completely different kind of
work, with a completely different kind of pressure.

Nine courses down. The certificate started by teaching me how systems
work, moved into how to watch them and automate that watching, and
closed by teaching me how to walk into a room and talk about all of it.
That's the full arc, start to finish.

**Is a week with no errors actually a quieter week — or just a week
where the mistake would have shown up somewhere other than a script?**
